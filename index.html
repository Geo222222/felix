My story

I’m Felix.

I’m Nigerian, and much of what matters to me is practical: helping people learn, creating with my hands, sharing my faith, and doing what I can to build a more stable life.

Right now I’m accepting support for basic living expenses while I continue developing the work I care about. Contributions create breathing room for immediate needs while I keep moving toward long-term independence.

I’m grateful for every person who contributes, shares this page, or simply encourages the journey.
